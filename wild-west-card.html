Wild West Race to the Top

Overview

  • Analogue Cardgame (2-4 player)

  • Deck based party game

  • Player aim to raise two stats (Macht/Ansehen aka Might/Prestige)to determine the winner

  • Player claim role cards influencing gameplay

  • Solo developed project

My Contributions

  • Game Systems Design

    1. Designed the Ruleset and winning condition
    2. Card-abilities of the role and ability cards played during turns
    3. Balancing of the abilities that let player hinder each other and the gain of the two winning stats
    4. Adjustment of the number of cards of each type within the deck

  • UI Design

    1. Visual design of the Monsters
    2. Created and placed the 2D hints

  • Level Design

    1. Visual design of each card and stat counter
    2. Physical creation of game asset
    3. Design of the four role characters to fit the setting (Wild West 1850)and to be diverse and interesting
