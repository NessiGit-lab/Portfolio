Hey there!

My name is Vanessa Kollburg, and I am a passionate game designer specializing in KPI-driven systems design, quality assurance, and the creation of reusable UI components.

My belief is that games allow a form of creative expression that no other media can imitate, and it is my goal to help with the structure of those ideas so they can truly shine.

I hold a Bachelor of Arts in Game Design and graduated with a focus on systems design. Throughout my career I became especially proficient in designing for browser- and mobile-based projects and helped by providing in-depth QA testing as well. Recently I broadened my education by completing a program about UI- and UX-design using Figma. This allowed me to gain experience in the creation of clickable prototypes and components for menu and design interfaces.

My aim is to improve myself even further to bring the unique world of games to life.
